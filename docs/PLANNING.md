# ReFeed - Project Planning Document

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** ReFeed

**Project Theme:** Campus Food Waste Reduction and Food Rescue

**Problem Statement:**  
Campus canteens often prepare more food than required because future meal demand is difficult to estimate accurately. This can result in unnecessary food preparation, food waste, and difficulty in redirecting safe surplus food to nearby community organizations.

ReFeed is designed to address this problem by connecting meal-demand forecasting, food preparation planning, meal operations, surplus detection, NGO dispatch, and impact measurement in one system.
